About Elite Drone Service

Pioneering The Skies with Cinematic Precision & Master Artistry

Founded by passionate aviators and visual directors, Elite Drone Service bridges the gap between state-of-the-art aeronautical engineering and high-end cinematic production.

Whether capturing the romantic grandeur of an imperial wedding, tracking high-speed action at outdoor festivals, or highlighting luxury real estate estates, our DGCA-certified drone crew operates with meticulous safety and unparalleled creative vision.

Dual-Pilot Safety & Redundant Systems

Equipped with obstacle avoidance, redundant GPS, and fail-safe return protocols for crowded venues and urban airspace.

Cinema Color Science & 10-Bit D-Log

Hasselblad and Zenmuse optical sensors delivering dynamic range, vibrant natural skin tones, and rich shadow detail.

Rapid 48-Hour Digital Cloud Delivery

Color-graded deliverables and organized raw archives uploaded to high-speed private cloud links ready for your timeline.
