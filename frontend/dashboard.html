Drone Regulations & Help Center (FAQ)

Standard operating guidelines compliant with Government of India Drone Rules

Q: How does DGCA Airspace classification work?

Green zones allow automated flight clearance up to 400ft. Yellow/Red zones (near airports or security installations) require prior Ministry clearance, which our flight operations team handles automatically on your behalf.

Q: What happens if it rains on the scheduled shoot date?

For flight safety and equipment protection, shoots cannot proceed during heavy rain or winds >25 knots. You can reschedule your booking free of charge using the "Reschedule Booking" action in your bookings tab.

Q: When and how do I receive my 4K aerial footage?

Raw 4K footage and color-graded deliverables are shared via secure Google Drive cloud link within 24–48 hours of flight completion.
